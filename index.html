Installation Guide

  1. Download Curve Factory after your license is issued.
  2. Extract the downloaded zip file.
  3. Copy the extracted CurveFactory folder.
  4. Paste it into your Adobe CEP extensions folder.
  5. Windows: C:\Program Files (x86)\Common Files\Adobe\CEP\extensions\
  6. macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/Adobe/CEP/extensions/
  7. Restart After Effects.
  8. Open Window > Extensions > Curve Factory.
  9. Enter the same email used here and paste your license key.

Updating Curve Factory

  1. Close After Effects.
  2. Download the newer Curve Factory zip from this page.
  3. Extract the new zip file.
  4. Replace the existing CurveFactory extension folder with the new one.
  5. Open After Effects again and launch Curve Factory.

Your saved presets and activation are designed to remain available after updates. Avoid resetting activation unless support asks you to.

Troubleshooting

  • If the extension does not appear, confirm the CurveFactory folder is inside the Adobe CEP extensions folder.
  • If activation fails, check that the email matches the one used to request the license.
  • If Google sign-in or verification fails, refresh the page and try again in a modern browser.
  • If the download does not start, wait a few seconds after license issue and click Download Plugin again.
